Authentic Homemade Mukhwas, Made Fresh

Enjoy Spoon brings you premium mukhwas online — a traditional Indian after-meal mouth freshener made the way it has been for generations. Every batch is hand-roasted in small quantities using wholesome grain and select Indian spices, with no artificial colors or preservatives.

Mukhwas is enjoyed across India after lunch and dinner to freshen breath and support healthy digestion. Our recipes keep that tradition alive with a clean, natural taste that feels just like homemade.

Gehu Mukhwas & Bajra Mukhwas

Choose from two signature flavors. Gehu mukhwas (Golden Grains) is made with roasted wheat for a mild, crunchy bite. Bajra mukhwas (Pearl Millet) uses nutritious bajri for a rustic, earthy flavor loved in Gujarati homes.

Both are light, crunchy and perfect as a healthy mouth freshener — for daily use, for guests, or as a thoughtful festive gift for Diwali, weddings and family celebrations.
